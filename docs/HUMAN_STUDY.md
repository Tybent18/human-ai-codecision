# Human Study Protocol Draft

[← Home](../README.md) · [Ethics](ETHICS.md) · [Evidence](EVIDENCE.md)

## Status

This is an implementation-ready protocol draft, **not institutional approval**. Do not recruit participants for publishable research until the relevant institution confirms whether IRB review is required and approves the protocol where applicable.

## Research design

- Target: 30–50 participants, powered formally before recruitment.
- Conditions: human-only, static recommendation, adaptive codecision.
- Assignment: randomized between-subject condition with balanced task difficulty.
- Trials: repeated cybersecurity classifications with ambiguity, complexity, and time-pressure manipulation.
- Primary endpoint: collaborative decision accuracy.
- Secondary endpoints: latency, appropriate reliance, unnecessary override rate, trust calibration, workload, satisfaction, and autonomy perception.

## Required additions before recruitment

1. Approved informed-consent and withdrawal language.
2. Inclusion/exclusion and compensation policy.
3. Power analysis and preregistered primary comparison.
4. Validated trust, workload, and satisfaction instruments.
5. Data-retention period, encryption, access roles, and deletion process.
6. Adverse-event and participant-contact procedures.
7. Accessibility and device/browser testing.

## Analysis plan

Report confidence intervals and effect sizes alongside inferential tests. Check model assumptions before repeated-measures ANOVA; use mixed-effects or robust alternatives when nesting, missingness, or non-normal outcomes make ANOVA inappropriate. Separate exploratory threshold tuning from confirmatory evaluation.
