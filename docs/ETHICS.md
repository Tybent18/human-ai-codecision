## Non-negotiable safeguards

- **Contestability:** participants can reject an AI escalation and request its reason.
- **Traceability:** every intervention records the state and thresholds that produced it.
- **Data minimization:** the pilot avoids direct identifiers by default.
- **Deletion:** session records can be removed through the study interface.
- **Bounded authority:** Stage One operates only on fictional cybersecurity classifications.
- **No high-stakes deployment:** this prototype must not control medical, legal, financial, military, aviation, or production-security decisions.

## Fairness evaluation

Behavioral uncertainty is not a direct cognitive measurement. Latency, cursor behavior, and revision patterns may vary with disability, device, language, familiarity, age, motor control, or interface accessibility. Before any demographic comparison, obtain ethical approval, minimize collection, test measurement invariance, and report false-intervention rates by relevant groups.

## Threat model

The system must be tested against overconfident AI errors, distribution shift, uncertainty-estimator error, over-intervention, automation dependence, trust collapse, adversarial telemetry, and unauthorized access to participant data.
