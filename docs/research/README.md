## Claim boundary

Synthetic Stage One demonstrates a reproducible, auditable authority-allocation pipeline under a controlled AI distribution shift. In the frozen configuration, adaptive codecision escalated 8.22% of decisions, overrode 0.33%, and produced 0.11% unnecessary overrides. It did not improve accuracy or declared utility over the human-only control.

These results do not establish improved real-world decision quality or measure human trust, satisfaction, workload, autonomy, safety, or cognitive state. Stage One included zero human participants. Participant claims require institutional review, informed consent, approved recruitment and retention procedures, and the validation program described in the ethics roadmap.
